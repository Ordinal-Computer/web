# Manifesto

Peter's words, dictated 23 Sep 2026. This is the canonical voice for Ordinal. Every
line of copy on the site answers to it.

Before this file existed the text lived only inside a session transcript, which is not
a place to keep the thing the whole brand is written from. Edit it here.

---

Ordinal is our attempt at something that may only be possible right now. In a few
years, the way people interact with computers will already have been set in concrete,
and most people will forget that there was ever a choice.

In a world where a person with an idea can outsource the doing to a machine and
receive a finished product, we were promised flight, and yet all we get are lines.
Modern systems simply lack the capability and compute and bandwidth to run at the
speed of thought, and so your thoughts are forced to wait. Thoughts that exist in
limbo for far too long become thoughts that you never had.

This cost is priceless. We believe it is most of what a person loses in their career.

On the outside, we may look like a cloud company, but we would disagree. We do not
rent machines and we do not own GPUs. We have the sole mission of saving people's
time, and large portions of their lives, so that they may work on meaningful things
and pursue the things that they value most at the speed of thought.

Our industry talks as though our history is five years long, and our world speaks as
though it will end tomorrow. Neither, we believe, is true. Everything being made right
now has followed something, and everything being made right now is what something will
follow.

Nothing you make here will be the first, and nothing you make here will be the last.
We believe that is real freedom.

We like to think that everything worth making is ordinal, and we hope you'll come make
the one after this, with us.

---

## How the site should sound

Taken from the piece above rather than invented.

- **Stakes before features.** The subject is what a person loses, not what the software
  does. A feature only appears once the loss has been named.
- **Declare, do not persuade.** "We believe", "we would disagree", "neither is true".
  No adjectives doing the work of an argument. Nothing is "powerful" or "seamless".
- **One concrete image per idea.** "Promised flight, and yet all we get are lines."
  "Set in concrete." Not a list of capabilities.
- **Short sentence, then a long one.** The rhythm is a flat statement followed by a
  sentence that unfolds. "This cost is priceless. We believe it is most of what a
  person loses in their career."
- **Say what we are not.** "We do not rent machines and we do not own GPUs." This is
  also why the site no longer names hardware.
- **The north star phrase is "at the speed of thought."** Everything else on the page
  is in service of it.
- No em dashes.
