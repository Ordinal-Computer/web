4.2 Input Content and Results

When you Boost a job, the Input Content that job requires is copied from your device to the Processing Systems so that the work can be executed there. Input Content may include source files, scenes, media, dependencies and configuration drawn from the project directory you are working in. After the first run, the Service ordinarily transfers only the parts of your project that have changed since the previous run, so a subsequent run of the same project ordinarily transfers less than the first. A full transfer may occur, for example where material previously stored is no longer held. Results are held on the Processing Systems while they are returned to your device, and thereafter for the purposes set out in sections 5 and 8.

Two consequences are stated expressly, because they are material and easily overlooked:

  • The Processing Systems must be able to read Input Content in order to execute it. The Service is not end to end encrypted, and we make no representation that User Content is unreadable to the systems that execute it.
  • The Service caches and deduplicates. Work that has already been performed may be answered from stored Input Content and Results rather than executed again, and identical material may be stored once rather than repeatedly. What you can observe is that an identical job returns faster and transfers less. This Policy does not represent that a cached item is isolated to your Account alone, and it does not represent that cached items are pooled across users, because neither statement should be made loosely in a document of this kind. What you may rely on is that User Content reaches us through jobs you Boost, that you may ask what we hold, and that you may ask us to delete it under section 13. If the answer is material to you before you Boost particular material, ask us at the address in section 19 and we will describe the behaviour of the build you are running. Until you have that answer, execute the work as Local Work. Section 11.4 of the Terms of Use states the same position, and the undertaking in section 7 of this Policy and in section 11.3 of the Terms is not narrowed by caching or deduplication: nothing here permits us to publish, sell, or make User Content available for browsing, or to use it to train machine learning models.

4.3 Command Text and Job Metadata

To execute a Boosted Job we Process the Command Text and the Job Metadata, which includes the tool and version invoked, arguments and settings required for execution, file names and paths within the project, sizes, timings, queue and exit status, resource usage, and which Processing Systems performed the work.

Command lines and file paths frequently carry more information than they appear to. A path may disclose a client name. An argument may disclose a credential if you place one there. Treat this category as content rather than as neutral telemetry. Assume that whatever a Boosted Job requires in order to execute may travel with it, including values drawn from the environment in which it runs. Do not place a secret in Command Text or in an environment value and then Boost it. If a build requires a credential, execute it as Local Work.

4.4 Diagnostics

The App may report crashes and errors. Diagnostics may include the App version, operating system version, a stack trace, and the state of the App at the time of failure. If you wish to know what the build you are running reports, or to have it stop reporting, write to us at the address in section 19 and we will describe what that build does and how to disable it where disabling is available.

4.5 Connection Data

Operating a networked service generates records of connections to it. Connection Data includes an internet protocol address, timestamps, request and response characteristics, and an identifier for the App build making the request. An internet protocol address constitutes Personal Data in some jurisdictions, and is listed here for that reason.

We do not operate advertising on it, we do not sell what it collects, and we do not construct a behavioural profile of you from it.
